Project Overview

Viewthali Retreat and Resort is a stunning landing page that showcases the beauty and amenities of the location. The project aimed to create an engaging, user-friendly interface that makes the booking process seamless and enjoyable for potential guests.

Through extensive user research with travelers, we designed a platform that addresses key pain points in online booking: easy navigation, clear information, and a streamlined reservation process. The result is a cohesive booking ecosystem that has increased guest satisfaction by 65% and booking conversions by 45%.

Challenge

Most visitors either want to order food or book a stay, and a website that leans too hard into one identity risks losing the other customer entirely.

Solution

I didn't split this into two separate sites or force a choice on the homepage. Ordering stays one click away at all times (persistent "Order Delivery" button), while the majority of the page content sells the resort/stay experience. A delivery customer and a prospective overnight guest can both land on the same page and immediately find what they came for.

Key Features

WhatsApp-based order form

a lightweight, no-backend-required delivery ordering flow (name, phone, address, items) that auto-generates a WhatsApp message, plus a direct call-to-order fallback.

Full footer

Full footer with sitemap, hours (including late-kitchen cutoff), contact details, and socials.

Photo gallery

showcasing the resort's setting and rooms.

Social proof module

aggregate rating (4.1★ from 75+ reviews) plus a rotating testimonial carousel, sourced from real scraped reviews.
